PVA synthesis {-}
------------------------------
Protocol for Succinic Anhydride coupling to PVA polymer:

![Reaction Scheme](PVA_succinic-anhydride_coupling.jpeg){width="6in" height="3in"}

# Materials: {-}
- DMSO, 50 mL plus wash
- Acetone, 200 mL plus wash
- PVA (any size, done with 15 kDa and 100 kDa), 5 g
- TEA (triethylamine), 0.7 mL
- hot plate with stirring function
- Oil bath
- stir bar
- spatula
- Pasteur pipettes
- round bottom flask
- filter paper
- Buchner funnel
- 50 mL Falcon tubes

# Procedure: {-}
#.
Heat an oil bath at 70 $^oC$
#.
Add 25 mL (saving the other 25 mL) of DMSO into a 250 mL round bottom flask
with a stir bar (stirring)
#.
Add 5 g of PVA with the desired molecular weight to the round bottom flask
#.
Using a 1 mL syringe add 0.7 mL TEA
#.
Using a Pasteur pipette, rinse the sides of the round bottom flask with the
remaining DMSO
#.
Lower the round bottom into the heated oil bath and leave to come to
temperature
#.
Add 500 mg of succinic anhydride after the flask is heated, can be added before
heating
#.
Leave heating for 2 hours for the reaction to occur (the PVA will dissolve
fairly quickly if not already dissolved)
#.
Raise round bottom out of oil bath to cool, wipe oil off of round bottom with
paper towels
#.
In a beaker with > 200 mL acetone, pour reacted solution in to crash out linked
PVA
#.
Use DMSO (with a Pasteur pipette) to rinse the stir bar and walls of round
bottom, pouring into the acetone beaker
#.
Using a spatula, break up the crashed out PVA and removing any pockets of DMSO
#.
Setup a Buchner funnel attached to vacuum with an acetone wetted filter paper
#.
Pour acetone with crashed out PVA into funnel
#.
wash PVA in funnel with more acetone
#.
Collect in funnel, transfer to falcon tube(s) and dry under nitrogen
